The institute is located in Sir Mokshagundam Visvesvaraya Road, Andhra Kempegowda International Airport is located at a distance of 190 kms away from the campus. Students can reach the campus by hiring a cab. The nearest railway station is the Anantapur Railway Station which is 2 kms away from the campus. The nearest bus stand is Anantapur Bus Stand, located at a distance of 5 kms from the campus. Students can easily hire an auto from the bus stand or a railway station to reach the campus. The institute has a well-equipped auditorium which is used for a variety of guest lectures and speaker sessions. The auditorium is also used to host a variety of cultural activities.
